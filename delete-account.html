Your data

Delete your account

Permanently remove your Click & Clique account and associated personal data. The fastest path is inside the app; use the form below if you cannot open it.

In the app

  1. Open Profile → Settings
  2. Tap Delete Account
  3. Read any penalty warnings, then confirm with your password

In-app deletion is immediate after confirmation.

Request by web

Email us from the address linked to your account. We process web requests within 7 days.

Submitting opens your email app with these details. You can also write us directly at support@clickandclique.com.

Read Privacy Policy

What we delete

  • Profile, photo, username, devices, and authentication
  • Chats and personal archives tied to your account
  • Active participations and invitations

Hosted community events with participants, and locked matches / joined community events within 24 hours of start, may apply system rating penalties disclosed before in-app deletion. A limited hashed-email reputation signal may be kept so re-registration cannot wipe ratings. See the Privacy Policy for details.
