Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Click & Clique (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application and related services (the “App”).

1. Who we are

Click & Clique helps people discover ticketable and user-created events in their city and connect with others who are going. For privacy questions, contact support@clickandclique.com.

2. Information we collect

Account & profile

  • Email address and login credentials
  • Username, country, state/city, time zone, birth date, gender, language
  • Optional profile photo
  • Interests and app preferences (for example notifications)

Activity in the App

  • Events you join or create
  • Interactions with other users
  • Messages you send
  • Reviews and ratings you give or receive
  • Device tokens used to deliver notifications you enable

Technical & local data

  • Technical information needed to operate the App (for example device and session data)
  • On-device data used to improve performance
  • Public event listings and similar catalog data, which are not your personal profile

3. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the App
  • Keep the product safe and fair (including abuse and spam prevention)
  • Send notifications you enable
  • Improve reliability and performance
  • Comply with law and respond to valid requests

4. Where data is stored

We store and process data using cloud service providers that host the App on our behalf. Data may be processed in the regions those providers operate in.

5. Sharing

Other users can see information you choose to make visible — for example your username and photo, and content you share with them.

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers (such as cloud hosting, authentication, and messaging providers) that process data on our behalf to run the App.

6. Retention

We keep account and activity data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal data as described below, except limited signals needed for abuse prevention or where law requires retention.

7. Account deletion

You can delete your account in the App: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. You can also request deletion on the web: Delete account page.

When deletion completes, we remove or anonymize personal data such as:

  • Your profile, photo, username reservation, devices, and login account
  • Your messages and other personal data tied to your account
  • Your event participation records

Names shown to others in historical contexts may be replaced with a non-identifying label (for example “Deleted User”).

Important: If you delete your account while you still have outstanding obligations under App rules, the App may apply consequences disclosed in the confirmation screens before deletion. A limited non-identifying signal may be retained so the same email cannot evade restrictions by re-registering.

8. Your choices

  • Update city, language, and profile details in Settings / Profile
  • Control notification preferences in Settings
  • Manage your activity in the App according to the rules shown there
  • Delete your account as described above

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age required by local law to consent to data processing without parental approval. Do not create an account if you do not meet the applicable age requirement.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, when appropriate, provide additional notice in the App.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: support@clickandclique.com
